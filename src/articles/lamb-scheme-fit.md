A receiver can be exactly as good as he was last season and still be worth materially less in fantasy. That is uncomfortable to write in a rankings post, because it looks like a talent judgment when it is not.

## What changed in Dallas

The new coordinator has pushed average depth of target down and leaned into yards after catch. That is a coherent offense. It is also a different job description than the one Lamb was drafted into.

## Why this shows up in the rankings

Scheme Fit compares a player's own profile against the tendencies of the offense he is in. When those diverge, production usually follows within a few weeks — which is why the ranking moves before the box score does.
