The waiver wire rewards people who move on trend rather than on results. These three all have Opportunity Trend Scores in the top fifteen at their position and roster rates that have not caught up.

## The pattern to watch

A rising OTS with a flat roster percentage is the cleanest signal available in a redraft league. It means the role is expanding and the market has not noticed, which is a window that usually closes within a week.

## What to do about it

Spend on the trend, not the box score. The players below all had unremarkable weeks by conventional measures and materially better weeks by role.
